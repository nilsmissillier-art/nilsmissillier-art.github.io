Bropacking trip

Annecy-Oslo

August 2026 • 15 days • 1900km

Just Two Bros, Two Bikes, and One Tent

Before diving into the day-by-day, let me set the scene. It was a raw bikepacking trip between two brothers, armed with just our bikes, a tent, and the absolute bare minimum of gear. Matt and I have always been massive outdoor junkies, and to be honest, we already got along pretty great before this. But surviving 15 days of headwinds, mechanical disasters, and sleeping on thin mattresses side-by-side brought us together. Welcome to the "Bropacking" trip!

DAY 1-4: Starting from Home

We kicked off this trip straight from our childhood home in Annecy. Our plan was to check out the North Sea coast before heading up to Sweden and Norway, which meant crossing northern France to reach Belgium. Day 1 in the Jura mountains (170km) hit us really hard thanks to the brutal elevation gain. Let me tell you, that first stop in Salins-les-Bains was an absolute lifesaver! Day 2 was long but smooth sailing. However, Day 3 had a nasty surprise waiting for us: Matt woke up with a vicious stomach bug that drained all his energy (couldn't eat a thing without... well, you know). Realizing we were completely out of gas — or "oxy complet," as we say — we swallowed our pride and caught a train to get closer to our bed in Belgium.

Day 4-8: Netherlands & the Crankset Disaster

After a solid day riding through Brussels, we pitched our tent in the Netherlands. The landscape changed drastically, and the Dutch bike paths are a dream to ride on! Our next goal was Haarlem to sleep at Ties' place, a long-time friend. But plot twist: just 30km from Haarlem, Matt discovered a massive crack in his crankset. We limped into Haarlem, but his bike was officially deceased. Luckily, we were in the bike capital of the world. So we had to stay an extra day waiting for a new crankset to be delivered. We took this forced "day off" to play tourists in Haarlem and Amsterdam with Ties. To make up for lost time, our solution was to hop on a 5 PM train right after the bike surgery, sleep in Dortmund, catch another morning train to Hamburg, and hit the road again at 10 AM on Day 8.

Day 8-10: A New Trip Start

By Day 8, our legs were itching to get back on the pedals. The scenery totally transformed again, treating us to incredible views as we hugged the coast north of Lübeck towards the Danish border. Day 9 greeted us with our first rainy wake-up call. Still, we were so hyped to cross into Denmark via a massive ferry that we pushed as far as we could, camping near Næstved. First flat tire of the trip, and the rain killed Matt's phone (turns out our "waterproof" pockets weren't always so waterproof...). Day 10 was a half-day ride because we wanted to explore Copenhagen. The tent stayed stuffed in the bag — tonight, we upgraded to a real hotel bed! We celebrated with a huge dinner with the Salomon Bags crew, sharing road stories.

Day 10-14: The Swedish Coast

After a huge breakfast and cruising along the incredibly beautiful Danish "Côte d'Azur," we hopped on another ferry and arrived in Sweden! Honestly, the Swedish leg was my favorite part. We're talking mind-blowing landscapes, iconic red wooden houses, fluorescent green grass, and stunning fjords. One night, we camped on a small farm field just 100 meters from the sea. The water was freezing, but a mandatory dip was totally worth it. Physically, these four days were tough: brutal coastal crosswinds with heavy bikes and hot weather. However, this was an amazing part of the trip!!!

Day 15: OSLO — The Finish Line

After tracing the edge of a gorgeous lake born from a fjord, we finally rolled into Oslo! Our mom was there waiting for us at the finish line. 1900 kilometers, a broken bike, a drowned phone, and a million memories later... we actually made it. Time for a well-deserved rest and a nice sauna...
